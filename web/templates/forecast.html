Economic Cost of Inaction (COI) & Escalation Ratio
Cost of Inaction (COI) = CostDelayed({{ forecast_data.days_horizon }}d) − CostToday
COI Penalty = {{ "{:,.0f}".format(forecast_data.total_cost_inaction_etb) }} ETB − {{ "{:,.0f}".format(forecast_data.total_cost_today_etb) }} ETB
Net Inaction Penalty = +{{ "{:,.0f}".format(forecast_data.total_cost_of_inaction_etb) }} ETB
Cost Escalation Ratio = {{ forecast_data.cost_escalation_ratio }}× (CostDelayed / CostToday)
This metric quantifies the financial penalty of deferring maintenance, NOT a return on investment (ROI). Delaying maintenance allows defects to expand into structural layers, requiring deep excavation, crushed aggregate rebuilding, and asphalt reconstruction instead of surface patching.
