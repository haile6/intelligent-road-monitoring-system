Contractor Liability & Warranty Policy (FIDIC & ERA)
Ethiopian Roads Administration Operational Framework
1. Defects Liability Period (DLP)
Construction firms are legally bonded to maintain roadway integrity for 24 to 36 months following official commissioning under standard FIDIC and Ethiopian Roads Administration (ERA) contractual covenants.
2. Computer Vision Evidence Verification
Every pothole and fatigue crack cataloged by the RoadEye neural pipeline is geotagged with sub-meter GPS and physical contact dimensions ($Z$), providing incontrovertible technical audit proof for liability assignment.
3. Automatic Rectification Orders
Formal notices include high-resolution optical captures, GPS coordinates, and a 14-day mandatory repair ultimatum before municipal back-charging occurs to the contractor's performance guarantee.
4. Future Tender Penalties
Contractors with persistent Grade D or F pavement scores receive automatic merit deductions in subsequent public highway bidding processes across federal and regional projects.
