Contractor Liability & Warranty Policy (FIDIC & ERA)
Operational Framework

1. Defects Liability Period (DLP): Construction firms are legally bonded to maintain roadway integrity for 24 to 36 months following official commissioning.

2. Computer Vision Evidence: Every pothole and fatigue crack cataloged by the RoadEye neural pipeline is geotagged with sub-meter GPS and physical contact dimensions ($Z$).

3. Automatic Rectification Orders: Formal notices include high-resolution optical captures, GPS coordinates, and a 14-day mandatory repair ultimatum before municipal back-charging.

4. Future Tender Penalties: Contractors with persistent Grade D or F pavement scores receive automatic merit deductions in subsequent public highway bidding processes.
