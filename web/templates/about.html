Operations Dashboard

RoadEye™: Intelligent Road Surface Monitoring, Predictive Pavement Maintenance & ADAS Hazard Intelligence Platform

An end-to-end Computer Vision, Multi-Vehicle Crowdsourcing, and Machine Learning decision-support system built for Ethiopian highway corridors (Route A2 · Addis Ababa – Debre Berhan) following ERA 2026, ASTM D6433, and FIDIC Clause 11 engineering standards.

Problems Addressed & System Solutions
Stakeholder Core Challenge RoadEye™ Solution
Drivers & Road Users Unmarked deep potholes and cracks cause tire/suspension damage, panic swerving, and preventable collisions—with no localized road-hazard alerts. Real-time YOLOv8 hazard detection combined with kinematic Time-to-Collision (TTC) and bilingual Amharic & English ADAS warnings.
Road Authority (ERA) Slow manual surveys, duplicate sightings inflating budgets, lack of standardized PCI/IRI metrics, and no ML forecast for rainy-season cost escalation. 8.0 m Haversine spatial de-duplication + Bayesian fusion, automated ASTM D6433 PCI/IRI & ERA BoQ, and Scikit-Learn ML deterioration forecasting.
Contractor Governance Roads failing prematurely within the Defects Liability Period (DLP) often lack timestamped GPS evidence to enforce contractor-funded repairs. Automated 5-stage Work Order Kanban and printable ERA / FIDIC Clause 11 Warranty Claim Notices with itemized repair liabilities.
End-to-End Engineering Pipeline
1. Vision & 3D Depth
YOLOv8 detects potholes & cracks; monocular 3D SfS estimates depth (Dmax) and cavity volume.
2. ANPR & ADAS Safety
Tracks dual-layer risk (Structural + TTC) and auto-computes heavy truck % from Ethiopian plates.
3. Fleet De-Duplication
8.0 m Haversine spatial clustering & Bayesian fusion merge repeat passes into verified hazards.
4. ERA BoQ & ML Forecast
Generates ASTM D6433 PCI/IRI, ERA 2026 BoQ costs, and HistGradientBoosting future forecasts.
5. Dispatch & DLP Claims
Tracks crew repair workflows and enforces FIDIC Clause 11 contractor warranty rectification.
