Live Video Feed & Mobile Device Integration

Hardware connectivity for USB cameras, mobile dashcams, and wireless tablet inspection

Wireless Mobile Phone Camera Stream (IP Stream)

Turn any Android phone or iPhone into an active vehicle dashcam transmitting real-time video to the neural detector over Wi-Fi or phone hotspot:

  1. Install IP Webcam (Android) or DroidCam / Live-Reporter (iOS/Android).
  2. Connect your phone and PC to the same Wi-Fi network, or enable your phone's Wi-Fi hotspot and connect your computer to it.
  3. In the app, start the camera server. The app displays an address like http://192.168.43.1:8080/video or http://10.18.60.X:8080/video.
  4. On your computer terminal, run the unified telemetry engine with your phone stream URL:
# Replace with your phone's IP address from the app
python run_integrated.py --source "http://<PHONE_IP>:8080/video" --show
Real-time potholes, cracks, distance, TTC & audio warnings will run live from your phone camera!
Direct USB Dashcam or Built-in Camera

Direct hardware interface to standard USB video capture devices (UVC protocol):

  1. Plug your USB dashcam or webcam into your computer.
  2. Run device index 0 (built-in camera) or 1 (external USB camera) with the --show flag:
python run_integrated.py --source 0 --show

Press Q in the video window at any time to finish and auto-save the telemetry session.

Access Web Panel on Mobile Phone Browser

Access the full management panel, GIS map, and telemetry studio from your phone's browser:

  1. Ensure your phone and this computer are connected to the same Wi-Fi network or phone hotspot.
  2. Open Safari or Chrome on your mobile phone.
  3. Type the following network URL into your mobile address bar:
http://10.18.60.191:8080
